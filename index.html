02Reminders

A quiet coach, not a nag.

Reminders are planned on your iPhone and written to make you smile, not to make you feel bad. Pick a gentle banner, a time-sensitive alert, or an alarm that rings through Silent Mode.

Switch day
On the morning your next tray is due, with two gentle follow-ups if it slips.
Back in
A set number of minutes after they come out. You choose how long.
Evening check
Optional. If nothing is logged by your check time, one nudge in case a meal slipped by.
Still out?
Forgot to tap Back in? Follow-ups after 24 hours, 48 hours and a week.

09Questions

Good questions.

More answers, including reminders, widgets and Siri, are on the support page. Or email the developer; every message is read by a person.

What is TrayTrack?

An iPhone app for people in clear-aligner treatment. It tracks how long your aligners are out each day, keeps your tray schedule, reminds you to switch and to put them back in, and keeps a dated photo record of your smile.

Does it work with my aligner brand?

Yes. TrayTrack works with any clear aligner. Your plan is just a number of trays, days per tray and a daily goal, so it fits whatever your orthodontist gave you.

How many hours should I wear my aligners?

Whatever your orthodontist asked for. Many plans ask for 20 to 22 hours a day; you set the goal and can change it any time. TrayTrack is a tracking tool and doesn't give medical advice.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no login. Open the app, set up your plan in under a minute, and start tracking.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud Sync (Premium), it's also stored in your own private iCloud so your other devices can see it. TrayTrack has no server and the developer can't see your data.

What happens when I finish my last tray?

TrayTrack asks what's next: refinements continue the same treatment with new trays, retainers start a retainer phase (full-time or nightly), or you can start a new treatment or simply finish.

What does Premium cost, and is it a subscription?

It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and you can try it free for 7 days. The price is shown in the app in your local currency. Purchases and refunds go through Apple.

Is TrayTrack on iPad, Apple Watch or Android?

TrayTrack runs on iPhone and iPad (iOS and iPadOS 26 or later) and has an Apple Watch app, which is part of Premium and works with your iPhone. There is no Android version.
