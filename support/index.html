01

Logging wear

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no login. Open TrayTrack, set up your plan (number of trays, days per tray and your daily goal), and start tracking.

How do I log my aligners out and back in?

On Today, tap the dial or Take aligners out when they come out. An out-timer starts and the ring shows how much of today's out-time allowance is left. When they go back in, tap the dial again or Put aligners back in.

You can also log from a widget, the Live Activity on your Lock Screen, Control Center or Siri (see Widgets, Live Activity & Siri).

I tapped by mistake. Can I undo it?

Yes. After each tap an Undo bar stays on screen for ten seconds. If it's gone, find the session in the list under the dial on Today and delete it.

What do the dial's colours mean?

The ring is today's out-time allowance (24 hours minus your goal). It drains as you use it, and turns amber when it's running low and red if you go over. The numbers are always the real ones; nothing is rounded up.

What's the week strip on Today?

Seven small rings, from six days ago to today, each filled toward that day's goal. A check means the goal was met. The header counts finished days only, so today never counts against you before it's over.

How do I change my plan or daily goal?

Tap the gear on Today to open Settings, then Treatment. You can change your tray count, current tray, schedule and daily goal at any time.

02

Trays & calendar

How do I log a tray switch?

Tap your tray on Today, then Switch to Tray N. On the last tray the button reads Complete Treatment. You can also say Log a tray switch in TrayTrack to Siri.

I switched too early. How do I undo a switch?

Open your tray from Today and tap Undo Last Switch. Completed treatment by mistake? Open your tray history from the Calendar tab and tap Reopen Final Tray.

What do the calendar colours mean?

Each day is coloured by how close it came to your goal: green when it was met, amber when it was close, red when it fell short. Tap a day to see its sessions, add a note, or add an appointment.

Can appointments go into my iPhone Calendar?

Yes. When you add an appointment, turn on Add to Calendar. TrayTrack asks for Calendar access so it can add the event and keep it up to date (or remove it) when you change or delete the appointment. It only works with the events it added.

How do I share a report with my orthodontist?

In Settings, open Compliance Report to make a wear summary as a PDF or text and share it however you like (Premium). The report is created on your iPhone and only goes where you send it.

03

Reminders

Which reminders does TrayTrack send?
  • Switch reminder: on the day your next tray is due, at the time you choose, with up to two gentle follow-ups on the next two days if the switch hasn't been logged.
  • Reinsert reminder: a set number of minutes after your aligners come out (you choose how many), to put them back in.
  • Evening Check (optional): if you haven't logged any out-time by your check time, one gentle nudge in case a meal slipped by. At most one a day.
  • "Still out?" follow-ups: if you take your aligners out and never tap Back in, TrayTrack checks in after 24 hours, 48 hours and a week. They stop as soon as you log Back in, delete that session, or finish treatment.

Change them in Settings → Reminders.

What are the reminder styles?

You can choose a gentle banner, a time-sensitive notification (it can show during a Focus), or an alarm that rings even when your iPhone is on Silent. The alarm style uses Apple's AlarmKit and asks for permission the first time.

My reminders aren't showing up.

Check that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings → Notifications → TrayTrack. If you picked the alarm style, also allow alarms for TrayTrack. TrayTrack shows a note in Settings → Reminders when either is turned off.

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone, so they work offline and nothing is sent to a server.

04

Widgets, Live Activity & Siri

How do I add a TrayTrack widget?
  1. Home Screen: touch and hold an empty spot, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for TrayTrack.
  2. Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose the Lock Screen, then tap the widget area.

Settings → Widgets in TrayTrack shows every widget with your own numbers.

Which widgets are there?
  • Today: the whole ring is a button. Tap it to take your aligners out or put them back in.
  • Trays: your current tray and when the next switch is due.
  • Next Switch: a countdown to your next tray. On the last tray it counts to the finish.
  • Finish Line: days to your projected end of treatment, with overall progress.
  • Lock Screen: worn today, next switch, and the Next Switch and Finish Line countdowns.

There's also a Control Center toggle for logging in and out.

What is the Live Activity, and what does Back in do?

While your aligners are out, a Live Activity on the Lock Screen shows the out-timer. Tap Back in on it to log them back in without opening the app; the card then goes away.

In the dimmed Always-On view it shows clock times instead of a ticking timer, for example "Since 3:32 PM".

What can I ask Siri?

These work straight away, with nothing to set up. Say the app's name as shown:

  • Aligners out in TrayTrack or Take my aligners out with TrayTrack
  • Aligners in with TrayTrack or Put my aligners back in with TrayTrack
  • Log a tray switch in TrayTrack
  • Add an appointment to TrayTrack and Add a note to TrayTrack
  • Check my wear time in TrayTrack
  • How much time do I have left out in TrayTrack
  • Which tray am I on in TrayTrack
  • When is my next tray switch in TrayTrack
  • When will my treatment finish in TrayTrack

The full list is in Settings → Siri. The same actions are available in the Shortcuts app.

05

Photos

How do I take a progress photo?

On the Photos tab, tap + and take a photo. A guide on screen shows where your teeth go ("Align your teeth here"), and TrayTrack keeps just that area, so every photo lines up with the last. Photos are filed by tray.

Can I import a photo and crop it?

Yes. Choose Choose from Library, then tap Crop on the photo. Pinch and drag to fit your smile inside the same teeth guide the camera uses, and tap Use Crop. Crop Again and Undo always start from the original photo.

How do I compare two photos?

With two or more photos, open Before & after to compare any two side by side and share the result (Premium).

Where are my photos stored?

On your iPhone, inside TrayTrack. If you turn on iCloud Sync (Premium), they're also stored in your own private iCloud and count toward your iCloud storage. They are never sent to the developer.

06

Premium & iCloud Sync

What's free, and what does Premium add?

Wear tracking, reminders, trays, the calendar with notes and appointments, your full history, photo capture, widgets, the Live Activity and Siri are free.

Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, with a 7-day free trial. It adds iCloud Sync, export of all your data, the compliance report, before & after compare, color themes (Ocean, Violet, Sunset), alternate app icons and the Apple Watch app.

Is there an Apple Watch app?

Yes, and it is part of Premium. It installs with TrayTrack on a paired Apple Watch; without Premium it shows a short message and nothing else. With Premium, tap the ring to take your aligners out or put them back in, see today's wear and your current tray, and add a watch face complication.

The watch works through your iPhone, so open TrayTrack on the iPhone once after buying Premium. If the watch says it hasn't updated, open TrayTrack on the iPhone and the two will sync.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in TrayTrack and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. After buying, TrayTrack shows Request a Refund in Settings, which opens Apple's refund form. You can also request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Premium ends, themes go back to Teal, the icon resets, sync pauses, and nothing is deleted.

How does iCloud Sync work?

Turn on iCloud Sync in Settings (Premium), then restart TrayTrack to apply the change. Your treatment, wear history, notes, appointments and photos are stored in your own private iCloud database, so they appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. The developer can't see them.

If sync can't run (for example, you're not signed in to iCloud), Settings says why, and your data stays safe on your iPhone.

07

After treatment

What happens after my last tray?

When you complete treatment, Today shows What's Next? with four choices: refinements, retainers, a new treatment, or all done. Your history and photos stay either way.

How do refinements work?

Choose I'm getting refinements and enter the new tray count and days per tray. The trays carry on from your last one (Tray 21, 22 and so on) in the same treatment, so your history, photos and report stay together. Set it up by mistake? Undo Last Switch on the first refinement tray takes the round back.

Can TrayTrack track my retainer?

Yes. Choose I'm moving to retainers and pick full-time (22 hours) or nightly (10 hours); you can fine-tune the goal later from the retainer row on Today. The dial, sessions, reminders, widgets and Live Activity all work as before, without tray switches.

08

Your data

Where is my data, and who can see it?

Everything is stored on your iPhone. With iCloud Sync on, it's also in your own private iCloud. TrayTrack has no server, no analytics and no ads, and the developer can't see your data. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?
  • One item: delete a session from the list on Today, a photo from the photo viewer, or a note or appointment from its editor.
  • Everything, from inside the app: Settings → Delete All My Data. Removes your treatment, history, photos, notes, appointments and reminders (and, with iCloud Sync on, from your other devices too). Purchases are kept.
  • Everything on this iPhone: delete the TrayTrack app. Its data is removed with it.
  • Everything in iCloud (if you used iCloud Sync): in iOS Settings, tap your name, then iCloud → Manage Account Storage → TrayTrack, and delete its data.
Does TrayTrack give medical advice?

No. TrayTrack is a tracking tool. It records what you log and reminds you of the plan you set. Always follow your orthodontist's instructions.

Accessibility

Does TrayTrack work with VoiceOver and larger text?

Yes. TrayTrack supports VoiceOver (the dial reads out whether your aligners are in or out and how much out-time is left), Dynamic Type up to the largest sizes, and Reduce Motion. Colours are never the only signal: goal status also uses a check mark or text.

If something is hard to use with your setup, please email me; accessibility problems are treated as bugs.
